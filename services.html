Frequently Asked Questions

When should I schedule my pre-wedding trial? ▼

We recommend scheduling your trial 8 to 12 weeks before your wedding date. At this point, your gown has been chosen, your veil is ordered, and you have finalized your jewelry and color palette. If you wish to align your trial with your bridal shower or engagement photoshoot, let us know when booking!

How long does bridal hair and makeup take on the wedding morning? ▼

We allocate 2 to 2.5 hours exclusively for the bride to guarantee a relaxed, unhurried experience complete with cryo skin prep and champagne breaks. For bridesmaids and family members, we allocate approximately 45 minutes per service.

Can you accommodate large bridal parties of 6 or more? ▼

Yes! For parties larger than 5, we assign secondary senior artists vetted and trained directly by our Lead Artist. This ensures everyone finishes on time without anyone having to wake up at 5:00 AM.

Do you travel on-location to hotels, private estates, and destination venues? ▼

Absolutely. We are fully mobile and arrive with professional illuminated vanity setups, high-temperature styling irons, extension cords, and directors' chairs. Travel within the NYC metropolitan area (up to 30 miles) is included in our signature packages.

How do I secure my wedding date on your calendar? ▼

Due to the intimate nature of our atelier, we take a limited number of bridal commissions per season. Dates are reserved on a first-come basis upon receipt of a signed contract and a 30% retainer deposit.
