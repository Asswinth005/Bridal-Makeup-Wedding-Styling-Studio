Founder & Lead Artist Isabella Moreau
Our Story

From a Single Vanity to a Beloved Studio

Isabella began her career as a fashion-week makeup artist, working backstage at Milan and Paris shows. After styling her sister's wedding, she realized the bridal industry needed something different — calm, precise, and utterly personal.

In 2014, Bridal Atelier opened its doors on Fifth Avenue. Today, our small team of artists has styled over 500 brides across four continents — from intimate city hall ceremonies to grand European estates.
